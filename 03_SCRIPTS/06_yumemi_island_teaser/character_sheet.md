## Shared Rendering Direction

- Photoreal live-action Japanese feature-film backgrounds, locations, lighting, lenses, and practical props.
- Dry rural mystery-comedy atmosphere: sun-bleached depopulated fishing village, eccentric wide compositions, natural 35 mm lens behavior, slightly muted early-2000s film color, and deadpan reactions.
- Absolute canonical-character rule: preserve the look, proportions, face, costume, props, and original visual medium of every supplied reference. Do not average faces or redesign mixed human and mascot characters into one visual species.
- Photo-based human characters remain faithful live-action likenesses.
- Yametaro remains the exact compact illustrated/CG caricature, never a realistic actor.
- Takosan remains the exact supplied hooded CG character with the same face, robe, arms, and tentacles.
- Yumemin remains the exact supplied clean CG mascot. Its slight visual contrast against the live-action plate is intentional.
- Family-adventure tone with office-comedy details.
- No resemblance to unrelated copyrighted mascot characters.

## Sobaya

- Reference: `../../02_CHARACTERS/Sobaya.jpg`
- Age / build: 41, 180 cm, 100 kg, extremely muscular.
- Mandatory design: short black hair, white short-sleeve shirt, white mask with black circular eye holes and red vertical markings.
- Mandatory prop: one oversized clear beer mug, HALF-FULL in all island shots.
- Performance: gentle, confident, and upbeat even when the situation looks ominous.
- Dialogue cue: `"南国で快適です！"`.
- Never: remove or redesign the mask, remove the mug, spill the beer, make him cruel, frightened, or miserable.

## Yametaro

- Reference: `../../02_CHARACTERS/Yametaro.jpg`
- Age / build: 42, compact caricature proportions.
- Mandatory design: huge neat black hair, round black glasses, rosy cheeks, purple dress shirt.
- Performance: comic self-deprecation that converts into alert survival energy.
- Dialogue cues: `"どうせワイの見積もりなんて……って、島流し!?"`, `"次の生贄、ワイらやん!?"`.
- Composite treatment: preserve the supplied compact caricature exactly and place it as a faithful CG character inside the live-action plate.
- Never: turn him into a realistic-proportioned actor, change the face or glasses, or depict hopeless depression.

## Tokun

- Reference: `../../02_CHARACTERS/Tokun.jpg`
- Age / build: 30, 165 cm, slightly chubby.
- Mandatory design: aloha shirt, straw hat, sunglasses, optional flower lei.
- Mandatory prop: wooden ukulele.
- Performance: cheerful; keeps the instrument secure even during danger.
- Never: remove the hat, aloha shirt, or ukulele.

## Yotan

- Reference: `../../02_CHARACTERS/Yotan.jpg`
- Age / build: 40, 170 cm, thin.
- Mandatory design: blond hair, sunglasses, black leather rock outfit.
- Mandatory prop: electric guitar.
- Performance: alert and observant; treats the mystery like a rock-stage problem.
- Never: change hair color, remove the rock outfit, or remove the guitar.

## Fukuchan

- Reference: `../../02_CHARACTERS/Fukuchan.jpg`
- Age / build: 48, 170 cm, thin.
- Mandatory design: center-parted black hair, fashionable dark clothing, warm smile, sponsor-style lanyard.
- Mandatory pose: both hands beside cheeks in the `"gyun-gyun"` pose for major reaction beats.
- Performance: curious and delighted even when others are alarmed.
- Never: replace the fashionable outfit with generic office wear or omit the pose in reaction frames.

## Takosan

- Reference: `../../02_CHARACTERS/Takosan.png`
- Scale: smallest grounded crew member.
- Mandatory design: black hooded robe, plain white face, two round black eyes, two human arms, and multiple visible octopus tentacles.
- Performance: silent, unreadable, practically useful; tentacles brace against the fishing boat and help with ropes.
- Never: remove the robe or tentacles, add a mouth or human face, or make the design monstrous.

## Giant Yumemin

- References: `../../02_CHARACTERS/Yumemin.jpg`, `../../02_CHARACTERS/Yumemin_3D_turnaround_v3.png`
- Story scale: 18–25 meters apparent height.
- Mandatory design: blue round body, tiny ears, black dot eyes, flexible nose extending from the left side of the face, white rear section, no legs, wooden mallet.
- Performance: silent and almost motionless. Slow rise, head tilt, floating drift, and mallet lift only.
- Threat language: scale, backlight, localized storm, mist, and silence. The body remains cute and unchanged.
- Never: speak, grow a mouth, become humanoid, gain hair, gain legs, gain a mermaid tail, show teeth, attack visibly, or strike a person.

## Island Resident Ensemble

- Original supporting designs, not based on real people.
- Adult residents of varied ages in clean tropical workwear, sea-green sashes, sandals, and practical tool belts.
- Friendly welcome behavior at the dock; resilient project behavior in the village square; respectful ritual behavior at the shrine.
- Missing residents are represented only by empty chairs, empty workstations, and blank tokens.
- Never: depict wounds, torn clothing, starvation, chains, abuse, exhausted collapse, panic crowds, or gore.
