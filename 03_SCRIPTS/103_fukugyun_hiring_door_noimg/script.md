### Fukuchan
PRESERVE: a slim 170cm 48-year-old Japanese man with adult human proportions; soft medium-length black
layered hair with a fringe that reaches his eyebrows; a warm gentle smile; an oversized black tailored long
coat worn open; underneath, a white T-shirt printed with black line art, black wide-leg trousers and white
sneakers; TWO things hang on his chest — ONE black lanyard printed with the word SPONSOR and ONE wide white
horizontal name card (keep the card blank, render no legible lettering on it). His signature pose, the
"gyun-gyun" pose, is BOTH hands closed into fists and pressed against BOTH cheeks.
do NOT carry over: pose, camera angle, sheet background, panel layout, text labels.

### Mob:knocker (the brass door knocker, not a person)
PRESERVE: ONE circular brass backplate with a horizontal barrel hinge across its top; a cast face of dark
patinated bronze bowl-cut hair with ONE sharp V-notch in the fringe over a pale polished brass face; SMALL
ROUND glasses with brass rims and WHITE lenses; TWO tiny dot eyes; ONE tiny dot nose; one small smiling
mouth; TWO round RED enamel blush cheeks; TWO round brass ear bosses at the sides of the head; ONE U-shaped
brass ring hanging from those bosses; ONE lavender enamel collar with a leaf pattern and a brass stud below
the face; ONE separate diamond-shaped brass strike boss below the assembly. Every part is solid metal —
brass, bronze and coloured enamel — never soft plastic and never painted skin. Only its eyes, brow and mouth
move; it has no body and never leaves the door.
do NOT carry over: the turnaround layout, the grey studio background, the arrow, the word labels.

### Okayaman
PRESERVE: a Japanese man with black medium-length mop hair whose fringe reaches his eyebrows; ONE mustache
joined to a chin beard; a calm gentle smile at all times; a black hooded jacket with a zip and a grey fleece
hood lining. He exists in this video ONLY as a live picture on a large flat display with a black bezel on a
stand — never as a body standing in the room (canon rule).
do NOT carry over: pose, camera angle, sheet background, inset close-up panels, panel layout, text labels.

### Mob:grand_door (set reference, not a person)
PRESERVE: a tall pair of dark red-brown stained oak double doors under an arched stone header carrying a
gilded brass fan ornament; THREE recessed panels on each leaf trimmed with gilded brass scrollwork; TWO long
vertical gilded brass pull handles meeting at the centre seam; the left leaf hinged on its FAR LEFT edge and
the right leaf hinged on its FAR RIGHT edge; on the middle panel of the RIGHT leaf, at shoulder height, ONE
round brass door knocker — a circular brass backplate holding a cast chibi face, with a U-shaped brass ring
