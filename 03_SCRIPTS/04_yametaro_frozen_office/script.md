# Yametaro's Frozen Office

## Production Summary

- Purpose: Opening cutscene for a fixed-camera 3D ice-sliding puzzle game
- Total target duration: 31 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Visual direction: cinematic stylized 3D animation with chunky voxel-inspired forms, readable silhouettes, bright office lighting shifting into crisp blue ice reflections, playful comedy rather than disaster
- Continuity: This is a new canonical incident after the existing timeline. Nothing is a dream.
- Tone rule: Yametaro made an honest, silly programming mistake. Nobody bullies or punishes him. The team immediately turns the frozen office into a rescue mission and enjoys the absurdity.
- Game objective established by the cutscene: recover every important document folder and every sealed beer case, then reach the emergency exit.

## Canonical Character Locks

- Yametaro: compact caricature proportions, large black hair, round black glasses, purple dress shirt, pink cheeks, comic self-deprecating energy. Do not redesign him.
- Sobaya: tall and muscular, mandatory white mask with red markings, short-sleeve white shirt, mandatory large beer mug. He is intimidating in silhouette but gentle and relentlessly positive.
- Okayaman: medium black hair, moustache and beard, black hooded jacket, calm smile. He appears only as a remote feed on a large office screen and never appears physically in the room.

## Global Prop Continuity

- Important documents: three thick blue folders with white paper visible at their edges. They begin neatly stacked, then slide onto separate ice tiles. They remain dry and closed.
- Beer cargo: two closed brown beer cases containing sealed upright bottles. No bottle breaks, opens, empties, or changes count during the cutscene.
- Sobaya's mug: one mandatory oversized glass beer mug, HALF-FULL from its first appearance through the final frame. He keeps it upright. No beer is poured or consumed and no liquid spills.
- Cooling server: a freestanding office server cabinet. It begins normal, develops frost at the vents, then remains frozen and glowing pale blue.

- Player character: Yametaro
- Core movement: one directional input sends Yametaro sliding in a straight line until he reaches a solid stopper.
- Required collectibles: all three important document folders and both beer cases.
- Exit rule: the red-lantern exit unlocks only after all required collectibles are recovered.
- Friendly assist roles:
  - Sobaya can smash one cracked ice block on designated stages.
  - Takosan can act as a soft tentacle stopper on designated tiles.
  - Yumemin can BONK a frozen switch to change partitions.
  - Tokun and Yotan provide rhythm and rock switch cues.
  - Fukuchan marks optional photo spots for score bonuses.
  - Okayaman explains each new regulation remotely.
- Ending beat: Yametaro fixes the cooling loop, every document is dry, the beer is extra cold, and the team laughs and shares a toast. Sobaya calls the accident `"メリットでもあります！"`.
