## Production intent

A job-hunting student at a careers fair asks Yametaro how to apply to "Yumemi Village". Yametaro gives him an
absurd secret procedure: order a steak set at the coffee shop on the ground floor of the AIT building, and when the
clerk asks "how burnt do you want it?", answer "low and slow". The student does exactly that, the clerk's eyes
glint, and he is led through the staff door into a dim service corridor — where the figure sitting in the dark
turns out to be Sobaya, who tells him he is hired and introduces himself as "Hisoba". Comedy, office slice of life,
everybody smiling at the end (WORLD_BIBLE Story Formula). No workplace-abuse, bullying or bleak content anywhere.

Note: the student's line says "アクセンチュア" rather than the canon fictional company "アクシデンチュア". This is a
one-off exception the user approved for this run only (2026-09-30). It is spoken audio only and never appears as text.
