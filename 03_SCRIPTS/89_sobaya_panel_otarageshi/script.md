# Otarageshi of the Window-Side Panel — Sobaya Smoke Finale

## Production intent

Create a 16:9 surreal but playful shrine-comedy video for Seedance 2.5 in CapCut. The supplied freestanding cardboard panel is placed before a shrine, then visibly sliced into smaller and smaller pieces, placed into a ceremonial fire pit and burned. The smoke gathers into one recognizable Sobaya face, then the face dissolves into ordinary smoke and disperses. Fukuchan performs the prayer in the user-requested shrine-inspired costume. There is no dialogue and no voice sample; use only the generated natural/action soundscape and no background music.

The supplied panel artwork is a physical prop. Its original printed masked figure, beer, meat and Japanese lettering may remain on the prop and its fragments. No new text, subtitles, captions, logos, watermarks or title cards may be generated inside Seedance.
